What We Offer ThinkLab Program

An onsite teacher training program delivered in your school, built on three phases.

1

Foundations Workshop

1 day immersive session. Teachers learn core thinking routines (See Think Wonder, Socratic questioning) and experience them firsthand.

2

Toolkit & Lesson Makeovers

4 weeks. Teachers receive ready to use strategies and redesign two of their own lessons to embed critical thinking.

3

Classroom Coaching & Peer Feedback

1 month. Our team observes, coaches, and helps teachers refine their practice so the change sticks.

No extra subjects. No overload. Just a smarter way to teach the existing curriculum.

The Gap is Real

In our own classes, we noticed discussions rarely move beyond finding the "right" answer. Teachers want tools to teach thinking but lack ready strategies. Globally, the OECD found that only 9% of 15 year olds can reliably distinguish fact from opinion – a basic critical thinking skill.

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is critical thinking?

It's the ability to analyze information, ask meaningful questions, evaluate evidence, and justify conclusions – not just memorize facts. ThinkLab helps teachers embed these skills into regular lessons.

Does this add extra work for teachers?

No. Teachers simply upgrade existing lessons by changing the type of questions and tasks they use. Our toolkit provides ready to use strategies, saving time in the long run.

Is this only for secondary schools?

We're piloting in grades 7 to 12, but the approach can be adapted to any grade level.

How long does the program take?

The full cycle is about 10 weeks, starting with a single Professional Development day, followed by short sessions and coaching.

What does the school need to provide?

We request a dedicated PD day and a small budget for workbooks/materials. We handle the rest.

Have you tested this before?

We've designed ThinkLab based on research and classroom observations. We're seeking a pilot school to measure impact and refine the program further. Pilot documentation is available in our leave behind packet.

How do we get started?

Contact us to schedule a 45 minute meeting and a brief demonstration workshop for interested teachers.
