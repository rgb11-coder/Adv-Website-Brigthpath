What is ThinkLab?

A teacher training program that transforms how classrooms think.

The Problem We Solve

Students are taught to memorize facts and follow instructions, but rarely to question, analyze, or create solutions. In a world that demands innovation and adaptability, this leaves them unprepared. ThinkLab was created to close that gap by equipping teachers with explicit methods for teaching critical thinking across all subjects.

Only 9% of 15-year-olds globally can reliably distinguish fact from opinion (OECD PISA 2018).

How ThinkLab Works

1

Foundations Workshop

A full-day immersive experience where teachers learn core thinking routines: See-Think-Wonder, Socratic questioning, and how to design open-ended challenges.

2

Toolkit & Lesson Makeovers

Four weeks of ready-to-use strategies. Teachers redesign two existing lessons to include critical thinking – no extra content, just better questions.

3

Classroom Coaching

One month of in-class support. Our team observes, gives feedback, and facilitates peer observation trios so new habits stick.

What Makes ThinkLab Different?

  • Practical, not theoretical. Teachers leave with ready-to-use tools, not just concepts.
  • Fits existing curriculum. No new subjects or textbooks – just smarter delivery.
  • Minimal disruption. Uses Professional Development days and short after-school sessions.
  • Measurable impact. Pre- and post-program student reasoning tasks show growth.
  • Creates a school-wide culture. When all teachers use thinking routines, students build the habit across classes.
